Calm Sort — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Calm Sort (“the app”) is a color-sorting puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we store on your device only

  • Your level progress, daily-challenge results, coins, boosters and settings (such as haptics and sound preferences) are stored locally on your device. They never leave it, and we cannot see them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows advertisements served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • Device identifiers (including, with your permission, the Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)
  • General device information (model, OS version, language)

How Google uses this data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the app asks for your permission before tracking, via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you still see ads — they are simply less personalized.

Consent (EEA / UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before personalized ads are requested, and your choices are respected. You can change your choice at any time from Settings inside the app.

Notifications

The app can send you one optional daily reminder about the Daily Challenge. It is off unless you turn it on in Settings, permission is requested at that moment, and you can turn it off again at any time.

Analytics

The app logs basic gameplay events (for example: level started, level completed) locally for debugging. In this version, no third-party analytics service receives your data.

Children

Calm Sort is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
